Stage 02 / Communication Layer

Skmsg

Messaging automation built after SkincareChart exposed appointment communication and follow-up bottlenecks.

Problem

Appointment communication remained separate from scheduling and staff operations, including reminders, cancellations, rescheduling, replies, and notifications.

Why It Mattered

Patient communication sits between clinical care and operations. Without connected scheduling data, templates, replies, and confirmations, the workflow depended on manual handoffs.

Design Decision

I designed Skmsg as a controlled messaging layer connected to appointment data, approved templates, inbound replies, and staff confirmation workflows.

What I Built

  • Twilio SMS/MMS sending and incoming webhook handling
  • Google Calendar appointment integration
  • Reminder, confirmation, cancellation, and rescheduling templates
  • Contact synchronization and selected Telegram confirmation flows

Technologies Used

Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, PostgreSQL, Railway, Twilio API, Google Calendar API, Telegram Bot API, Cloudflare/DNS.

What I Learned

Healthcare automation needs guardrails, confirmation steps, deployment discipline, templates, logging, and failure handling.

Next Problem Discovered

Staff needed faster operational access, which led to Telegram CRM Bridge.
