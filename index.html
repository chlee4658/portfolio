Telegram CRM

Staff Ops

Telegram CRM Bridge

Problem
Staff needed faster lookup and controlled messaging.
Built
Telegram lookup, approved messages, confirmations, and audit controls.
Stack
Node.js, TypeScript, Telegram Bot API, webhooks, feature flags.
Journey notes
Why it mattered
Staff tools must be quick while preserving permission boundaries and privacy-conscious logging.
Design decision
I separated lookup from write actions and added confirmations, audit behavior, and reversible rollout controls.
What I learned
Staff-facing automation needs predictable interactions, fallback paths, and clear operational boundaries.
How it led forward
This completed the first loop from documentation to communication to staff operations.
